SSRF escalation — decision tree

The exact path through the CV-upload / create-admin labs, as a flow.

1 · Does a feature fetch a URL you control?
Webhook, import, avatar/CV, renderer, link preview. Confirm with a listener hit. No hit → not SSRF.
2 · What's the fetcher? (read the User-Agent)
curl → gopher + redirects. python-requests → no gopher. Headless browser → file://, rendered internal pages.
3 · Can you reach internal services?
Denylist on localhost/127.0.0.1? Bypass with 0.0.0.0, 0, [::1], 127.0.0.2, 2130706433. Confirm against an internal-only route.
4 · Is the target endpoint a plain GET?
Yes → point the fetch at it. No (needs POST + body) → step 5.
5 · Forge the POST with gopher
gopher://127.0.0.1:80/_ + raw request. Content-Length exact, %0d%0a CRLFs, blank line before body.
6 · Is your payload inside a form field?
Yes (cv=gopher://…) → decoded twice. Double-encode: %→%25, &→%26, inner =→%3D.
7 · Verify out-of-band
gopher is blind — don't trust a reflected status:ok. Confirm on a public endpoint (log in as the admin you created).
